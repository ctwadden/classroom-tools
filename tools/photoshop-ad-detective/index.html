What should you be noticing?

These prompts are also in your Google Doc. Use them as a lens while you explore.

  1. What is being advertised, and what is the main idea?
  2. What part would be impossible or difficult to photograph normally?
  3. What different photographs or elements were probably combined?
  4. Where can you see evidence of image manipulation?
  5. What techniques do you think the designer needed?
  6. What makes the composite believable: light, shadows, colour, scale, perspective or edges?
  7. What does your eye notice first, and why?
  8. Would the ad still work without words?
  9. How creative is the idea? Defend your rating.
  10. Could you invent a different impossible image for the same product?
