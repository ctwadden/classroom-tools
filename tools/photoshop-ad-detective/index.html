Simple idea · strong execution

Fanta — Fruit + Can

A concept campaign by Gregorio Manetti. The project description explains that the top of a can was combined with fruit and finished in Photoshop.

Look for: clean selections, shape matching, highlights, texture, splash effects and colour consistency.

Surreal retouching

Splashdown Waterpark

The visual joke exaggerates what an intense waterslide might do to you.

Look for: body/clothing manipulation, masks, careful edges and believable lighting.

CGI + retouching

Sherlock — Stay in the Shadows

Light itself becomes the “thief,” pointing toward an unprotected car while the protected vehicle remains unnoticed.

Look for: lighting direction, colour grading, atmosphere, compositing and visual hierarchy.

Photomontage process

Sun Magic — Orange Juice

A Photoshop photomontage built from multiple separate visual elements. The project page includes process and before/after material.

Look for: cut-outs, scale, perspective, colour matching, overlaps and edge cleanup.

2026 Photoshop montage

Python Tape — Space Composite

A 2026 advertising photo-montage exercise that combines many source images into one deliberately impossible scene.

Look for: multiple source images, scale, reflections, shadows, colour balance and depth.
