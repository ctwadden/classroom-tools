Final product

Answer these in a Google Doc

Title your document Multimedia Futures 2030 — [Your Name] — [Career]. Answer in complete sentences and include source links.

  1. Your career: What multimedia role are you investigating, and why does it interest you?
  2. 2026 reality: Describe three real tasks this person performs.
  3. Real opportunity: Find one current job posting, studio profile or professional listing. Give the employer, title and location.
  4. Tool stack: What software, hardware or production technologies are expected? Identify at least three.
  5. Portfolio: What would an applicant need to show in a portfolio or demo reel?
  6. Training: What education, certification, self-training or industry experience is commonly expected?
  7. Money and work model: What pay information can you find? Is the work staff, freelance, contract, remote, project-based or mixed?
  8. Nova Scotia / Canada outlook: What does Job Bank or another reliable source say about the closest matching occupation?
  9. AI: Identify two tasks AI could speed up, automate or assist with.
  10. Human advantage: Identify three things a person still needs to contribute: taste, storytelling, directing, visual judgment, ethics, communication, client understanding, etc.
  11. 2030 workflow: Identify two technologies or trends that could significantly change this career by 2030.
  12. Emerging role: Find one newer or hybrid multimedia job connected to your career. What makes it different?
  13. Skill stack: Choose five skills a Grade 12 student should build now: two creative, two technical and one human/communication skill.
  14. 2026 → 2030: Complete and explain: “In 2026 this creator mainly ________, but by 2030 I predict they will increasingly ________.”
  15. Recommendation: Would you recommend this path to a student entering multimedia? Answer Yes, No or Maybe and defend it with three pieces of evidence.
  16. Sources: Paste at least three links you actually used, including the real job posting or profile.
