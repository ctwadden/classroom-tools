# OBSERVATION / CONVERSATION / PRODUCT RUBRICS

Common scale: 4 Transfer/Extend; 3 Competent/Independent; 2 Developing; 1 Guided/Emerging; IE Insufficient Evidence.

These are evidence lenses. Each project supplies task-specific look-fors.

## Observation
Process control; independence; troubleshooting.
4 adapts/transfers and diagnoses independently.
3 performs expected workflow independently.
2 needs prompts/checkpoints.
1 needs substantial guidance.

## Conversation
Explanation; reasoning/evidence; transfer/prediction.
4 uses accurate reasoning and transfers to a new context.
3 accurately explains what/why in the assigned context.
2 explanation is partial/general.
1 mainly repeats directions or needs substantial prompting.

## Product
Technical execution; communication/design; output/brief.
4 strong, editable work with justified transfer/refinement.
3 meets expected technical/design/output standard.
2 partial/inconsistent evidence needs repair.
1 major requirements missing or dependent on guided completion.

Do not infer a level from missing work alone and do not automatically average all evidence.
