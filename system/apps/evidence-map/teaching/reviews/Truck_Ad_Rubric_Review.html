TA-MM-C1 · MM12-1.1 · MM12-1.3

Editable image construction

Performance descriptors
BeginningThe intended composite is incomplete or visible edge, placement or editability problems prevent its use.
DevelopingThe image is assembled and partly editable; visible edge or treatment inconsistencies remain at the intended output size.
SecureThe composite reads coherently at the intended size. Masks and adjustment controls preserve the source and allow a targeted revision; the learner locates and explains those controls.
ExtendingThe learner adapts the editable construction to a substantially changed source or constraint and demonstrates why the revised method better meets that condition.

Collect: Product: layered master and export. Observation: locate and revise one controlling mask or adjustment. Conversation: explain why the edit is reversible.

Form choices: Smart Filter mask repair; Blend-mode reasoning; Inspect the layered product at 100%

TA-MM-C2 · MM12-1.4

Hierarchy and visual design

Performance descriptors
BeginningThe intended first-read message is unclear or essential text cannot be read at the required size.
DevelopingThe main message is visible, but type, contrast, spacing or competing elements weaken the reading order.
SecureType roles, scale, contrast and spacing establish a purposeful reading order. An audience read test supports the final revision.
ExtendingA changed audience or format requires a materially different hierarchy; the learner compares alternatives and uses audience evidence to justify the adaptation.

Collect: Product: original and revised export. Conversation: identify the first read and point to the design choices that create it.

Form choices: Typography / hierarchy revision; Final advertisement; Critique and revision

TA-MM-C3 · MM12-1.5

Communicating a message for an audience

Performance descriptors
BeginningThe visual treatment and words do not yet communicate the intended message to the specified audience.
DevelopingSome choices fit the audience and purpose, but the explanation does not connect those choices to their likely effect.
SecureImage and words work together for the stated audience and purpose. The learner supports the design explanation with visible evidence and a purposeful revision.
ExtendingThe learner evaluates two plausible treatments under a changed brief, explains a trade-off and identifies when the rejected alternative could be more effective.

Collect: Product: independent variant. Conversation: a 60–90 second changed-brief defence.

Form choices: Audience / message defence; Final advertisement; Critique and revision
