# DESIGN LEARNING PROGRESSION

The Technology Learning System is deliberately linear.

## Stage 0 — Design + Software Bootcamp
Core software mechanics plus introductory design principles:
- contrast
- hierarchy
- alignment
- proximity
- repetition
- colour
- typography

## Stage 1 — Technique Applications
Small problems combine 2-3 known skills. Students begin explaining why techniques work.

## Stage 2 — Guided Advertising / Applied Builds
Truck Standard projects. Verified workflows, professional parallels, outcomes and Obs/Con/Pro evidence are built in.

## Stage 3 — Artificial Client + Branding
Students receive a client, demographic, brand attributes, goal and constraints. Assessment shifts toward reasoning, influence, audience and message.

## Stage 4 — Campaign / Cross-media
One idea is adapted to multiple deliverables while preserving brand consistency and audience intent.

## Stage 5 — Portfolio / Independent Production
Students plan, prototype, revise, defend and present original solutions. Level 4 evidence requires independent transfer and troubleshooting.

## Principle
Software skill is the foundation. Design reasoning becomes progressively more important as students move from Bootcamp into client and campaign work.
