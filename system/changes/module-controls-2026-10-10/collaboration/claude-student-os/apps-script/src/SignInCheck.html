Check your school account

Open this page while signed in with your own school Google account. This checks your account against the class roster before the workspace opens.

It records an account-check time for your teacher. It does not load your work, marks or anyone else’s information, and it does not turn class access on.

If Google cannot identify you, use a browser profile signed in only with your school account, then retry. Never share your password.
